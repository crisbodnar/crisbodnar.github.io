Cristian Bodnar

Founding Member @ Prometheus. Co-founder @ Silurian AI. Previously @ Google Brain, MSR, Twitter Cortex.

I’m a Founding Member of Prometheus, where we are building general AI for the physical world.

I’m also a cofounder of Silurian AI. Previously, I was a technical co-lead at Microsoft Research for the Aurora foundation model—the first foundation model for the Earth System. Aurora brings together high-resolution weather, atmospheric chemistry, waves, and hurricane dynamics under a single AI model.

Before that, I completed my PhD at the University of Cambridge, under the supervision of Prof. Pietro Liò and as a recipient of the Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship (2021). I’ve spent significant time in industry and research labs, including stints at Microsoft Research (2022), Twitter Cortex (2021), Google Brain (2020), and as an AI Resident at Google X (2019). Earlier, I graduated with distinction from Cambridge’s MPhil in Advanced Computer Science, earning the Best MPhil Student Award.
